Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AVIGRONIX TECHNOLOGIES ("we", "us", "our") operates avigronix.com and its related services. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Contact form: full name, email address, phone number, company name, service of interest, and your message
  • Business/shop registration: shop name, business category, contact details, physical address, GST number (optional), social media links, payment/bank details (optional, if you choose to enable online payments for your shop), and uploaded logo/banner images

Information collected automatically:

  • Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring page)
  • Analytics data via Google Analytics (see "Cookies & Analytics" below)

2. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (pages viewed, session duration, general location and device type). Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect data in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. You can opt out using your browser's cookie settings or tools such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request
  • To operate business/shop pages you register with us, including their public subdomain storefront
  • To send transactional emails (e.g. acknowledging a contact form submission)
  • To monitor, secure, and improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only with the service providers needed to operate our platform:

  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics
  • Google (Gmail SMTP) — for sending contact-form and transactional emails
  • MongoDB — our database provider, used to store shop registration data
  • QR code generation service — used only to generate a scannable QR code for your shop's public URL, from the URL itself (no personal data is sent)

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of AVIGRONIX TECHNOLOGIES or others.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are used to respond to your inquiry and are not retained beyond what's needed for that purpose and our legitimate business records. Shop/business registration data is retained for as long as your shop remains registered with us, or until you request deletion.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below — we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including transport encryption (HTTPS), access controls, and secure credential storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Visitors

We serve clients across India, the US, Europe, and the Middle East. Your information may be processed in India, where our infrastructure and team are based. By using our site, you consent to this processing.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
