Project & Timeline

What is your typical project timeline?

Timelines depend on the complexity and scope:

  • Small websites: 2–4 weeks
  • Custom web apps: 4–12 weeks
  • E-commerce platforms: 6–16 weeks
  • Mobile apps: 8–20 weeks
  • Enterprise solutions: 12+ weeks

A detailed timeline is shared after the discovery phase.

What is your development process?

We follow an agile, transparent process:

  1. Discovery: Understanding requirements & goals
  2. Design: UX flows, UI design & architecture
  3. Development: Iterative builds in sprints
  4. Testing: QA, UAT & performance checks
  5. Deployment: Production rollout & monitoring
  6. Support: Maintenance, updates & improvements
How do you share project progress?

We keep you updated throughout the project via:

  • Weekly progress calls & demos
  • Access to project management tools (e.g., Jira, Trello)
  • Regular sprint reviews
  • Direct communication with project manager / lead developer
